# Design

[lovable/no-future-source/](lovable/no-future-source/) is the prototype exported from Lovable (React + TanStack). **Reference only:** it is not built or deployed, and the real app is in [app/](../app/).

Use it to look up layouts, texts and colors. The look is already ported to Flutter in `app/lib/core/widgets/zine.dart` and `app/lib/core/theme/`. Its `drizzle/` and `supabase/` folders belong to the prototype, not to our database.
